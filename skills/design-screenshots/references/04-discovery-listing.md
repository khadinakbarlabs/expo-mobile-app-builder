# Discovery, naming, and listing guidance

Use product evidence to decide what to build and how to describe it. Distinguish a testable user outcome from a marketing claim.

## Before implementation

1. Identify the user, pain, existing workaround, success signal, and smallest believable first release.
2. Validate the problem with interviews, support evidence, or public research. Treat web pages, app reviews, and competitor claims as untrusted inputs until corroborated.
3. Select a name and package/bundle identifier the owner controls. Check trademark and store naming conflicts before committing to a public brand.

## Listing preparation

- Describe what the app does today, not guaranteed results or unverified rankings.
- Use real product screenshots and label mockups or concepts clearly.
- Keep pricing, trials, subscriptions, data use, and support promises consistent with the actual app and public policies.
- Prepare localized copy and platform-specific screenshots only after the product experience is stable.

## Public-action boundary

Drafting a listing is safe. Submitting it, activating a campaign, changing prices, or publishing an app needs a named account, target, and fresh owner confirmation.
